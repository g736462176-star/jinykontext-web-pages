Temporarily under a pseudonym

Jiný Kontext is the work of one person who is both the author and owner of the project and currently publishes as V. Their identity will be disclosed at the announced time shown above. Until then, the website does not publish their legal name, address or other personal identifying details.

How the publication works

Jiný Kontext is the product of one author and owner, not a multi-person newsroom. Articles appear in three languages — Czech, English and German; the name Jiný Kontext is not translated. Sources are listed on the article page. Material corrections remain visible; the rules are in the editorial principles.

Project contact

Editorial questions, corrections and tips can be sent to jinykontext@proton.me.

Website operation

This public build is delivered through Google Firebase Hosting. The provider describes its processing of operational data in its privacy documentation.

Funding and commercial relationships

The current public website contains no advertising network, affiliate measurement, paid membership gate or profiling analytics. Any paid partnership or relationship material to assessing an article must be disclosed with the affected content.

Conflicts of interest

The author will disclose financial, professional or personal ties that could reasonably affect perceptions of independence. A partnership must never be hidden inside editorial content or change the stated strength of evidence.
